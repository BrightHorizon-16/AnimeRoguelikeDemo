CURRENT STATE
-------------
The clean starter prototype has one larger shared base with a timber frame and
no walls, surrounded by an animated rectangular conveyor. The map has bright
green studded ground, a red approach path, and a spawn plaza.

The screen GUI has a scrollable placeholder Shop, a scrollable Inventory with
live counts, and a Lucky Spin. Shop goods and 12 spin prizes are text-only
generic Roblox-style items. A spin costs 50 test coins, shows 15 passing cards,
and awards a random item selected on the server. Players start with 500 test
coins and get 10 more every 8 seconds. Hold Left Shift or Right Shift to
sprint (16 normal / 24 sprint speed). A controls guide stays at bottom-left.

The conveyor has a dark belt, rounded corners, and 144 thin animated crosswise
treads. It travels at 12 studs/second (twice the earlier speed) and carries the
player around its loop. The GUI follows a compact Roblox-style layout: backpack
button at top-left, Shop and Spin at left, coin icon and total at top-right,
nine-slot hotbar at bottom, and discreet text-only controls in the bottom-left.
Items are still placeholders and have no gameplay effect.

RUNNING IN ROBLOX STUDIO
-----------------------
In PowerShell:
  cd C:\Games\AnimeRoguelikeDemo
  & 'C:\Games\rojo.exe' serve default.project.json --port 34873

Keep Rojo running, connect the Studio plugin to 127.0.0.1:34873, sync, stop any
current Play session, then press Play again. Map source is
src/server/Main.server.lua; interface and sprint controls are
src/client/Main.client.lua. Continue using port 34873.
